5 Fixes That Can Turn a Business Profitable in 30 Days

Most startups and small businesses are stuck in survival mode — but profitability is possible, even fast. Here are 5 fixes that can drastically improve your cash flow in just 30 days if applied correctly.

1. Audit Your Offer & Pricing

Your product or service might be great, but if it’s priced wrong or poorly positioned, it won’t sell. Repackage your offer, add bonuses, raise prices for premium clients — or create bundles for value buyers.

2. Build a Sales Funnel

Stop selling cold. Create a funnel with a free offer (like a consultation or guide), capture leads, and nurture them through email or WhatsApp until they’re ready to buy. Automation helps here.

3. Fix Your Website Conversion Rate

If you get 100 visitors but no sales, you don’t need more traffic — you need a better website. Use clear call-to-actions, testimonials, and benefit-driven headlines. Mobile-first always!

4. Optimize Expenses

Audit your monthly burn rate. Cut tools you don't use, shift to freelancers instead of agencies, and renegotiate vendor rates. Every ₹1 saved = ₹1 profit earned.

5. Retarget Old Leads & Customers

Your most likely buyers are those who’ve already shown interest. Create a WhatsApp follow-up script, run retargeting ads, and bring back the warm audience sitting in your old lists.
